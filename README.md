
# Animal Farm Wiki

On a quiet farm in the English countryside, the animals dreamed of a better life.
Tired of working under the cruel farmer Mr. Jones, they decided to rise against him and take control of their own destiny.

At first, their dream seemed to come true. Every animal was equal, and the farm belonged to them all.
But as the seasons passed, the pigs began to seek more power, and the promise of freedom slowly faded.

Written by George Orwell, *Animal Farm* is a powerful tale of hope, betrayal, and corruption.
It reminds us how easily a beautiful dream can turn into a nightmare when power falls into the wrong hands.
