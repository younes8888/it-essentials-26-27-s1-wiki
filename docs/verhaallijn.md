# The Story of Animal Farm

## A Dream of Freedom

On Manor Farm, the animals live under the control of Mr. Jones, a careless farmer who often neglects them. Their days are filled with hard work, hunger, and little hope for a better future.

One evening, Old Major, a respected old pig, gathers the animals in the barn. He shares his dream of a world where animals live freely, without humans controlling their lives. His words inspire them to imagine a future built on equality and friendship.

## The Rebellion

After Old Major's death, the animals begin preparing for change. Led by the pigs, they eventually rebel against Mr. Jones and drive him away from the farm.

They rename Manor Farm to Animal Farm and establish the Seven Commandments, promising that all animals will be treated equally.

For the first time, the animals believe their future belongs to them.
## The Rise of Napoleon

As time passes, the dream of equality begins to fade. Napoleon, a powerful and ambitious pig, drives Snowball away and takes complete control of Animal Farm.

With the help of Squealer, Napoleon changes the rules and convinces the animals that everything he does is for their own good. Meanwhile, Boxer and the other animals work harder, believing their sacrifices will lead to a better future.

## A Dream Betrayed

The pigs slowly begin behaving like the humans they once hated. They sleep in beds, trade with humans, and enjoy privileges that the other animals cannot have.

By the end, the animals look through the farmhouse window and can no longer tell the difference between pigs and humans.

The dream of freedom has become a nightmare.
